Building intelligent autonomy through precision hardware.

Robotics shouldn't end at the code, and mechanical design shouldn't stop at CAD. I love bringing the two together — there's nothing quite like watching an abstract mathematical model spin up on physical hardware and conquer the real world.

Pure software is fascinating, but it really comes alive when paired with the physics of motors, linkages, and structural dynamics. I build end-to-end: first-principles autonomy paired with hands-on mechanical craft, so every system runs seamlessly.

What I get excited about

Autonomy & Dynamic Motion

Precision Machining & FEA

5-axis CNC toolpaths, custom adapters, and dynamic stress validation in the shop.

Rugged Mechatronic Packaging

Optical metrology, active sensors, and electronics in IP67-sealed field enclosures.
